04 / FUEL YOUR BODY

Nutrition & recovery

Build meals around everyday foods, enough energy, protein, carbohydrates, healthy fats and variety.

🍳

Breakfast

Eggs or yogurt, bread or oats, fruit and milk.

🍗

Lunch

Chicken, fish, beans or lentils with rice, potatoes or bread and vegetables.

🍌

Snack

Fruit with yogurt, milk, nuts or a cheese sandwich.

🥔

Dinner

Eggs, tuna, lentils or chicken with potatoes, bread and vegetables.

Important: This is a flexible meal guide, not a calorie-restricted diet. Weight, height and age alone cannot determine the right calories or whether a young person should gain or lose weight. If you are under 18, focus on regular balanced meals, growth, sleep and strength—not dieting or supplements. Ask a healthcare professional or registered dietitian for individual nutrition targets.

Daily recovery basics

  • Drink water regularly.
  • Eat regular meals and snacks when hungry.
  • Include a protein food in meals.
  • Sleep 8–10 hours if you are a teenager; adults generally need 7–9.
  • Take rest days seriously.

Before and after training

Before: Have a normal meal or snack that sits comfortably, and warm up for 5–8 minutes.

After: Have a balanced meal or snack, drink water and allow muscles to recover.
